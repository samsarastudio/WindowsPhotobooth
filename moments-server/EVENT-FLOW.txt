Haunted Hoops automatic event flow

Deploy the moments-server branch before starting the matching Event build.
Existing live settings are preserved (currently 4 shots, 200 points, Normal).
New installations default to those values. The two-photo price is CAD $10.

Guest flow
- Scan the game's registration QR and enter name, date of birth and consent.
- Your phone immediately displays your player QR. Scan it at the physical game
  scanner, then stand on the marker and follow the hand-selection instructions.
- No queue, turn call or phone confirmation is required.
- Play four scored shots. Below target: scan the same QR for a free retry from zero.
- Play free. Score the target to unlock 2 photos at just CAD $10.
  Registration, attract and results explain: visit the booth, pay $10, get photos.
- Host payment confirmation is required before two photos are credited.
- Presence controls idle signage / gameplay preview, not game admission.
- Historical recovered/completed attempts can be acknowledged on reset without
  affecting replacement games or refunding again. Unknown attempts remain blocked
  for host review; the recovery record is not silently deleted.
- F9 preserves result delivery and cancels unfinished free games without crediting money.
- Existing paid balances and interrupted game recovery are retained.
- Physical scanner, sensor placement and HDMI display require onsite acceptance.
