Mosaic wall

Guest photos collage on a filled wall with motion. At the end of the show, switch to a completed filled grid. Backdrop artwork sits in the middle of the wall and peeks through when photos swap. Brand partner reveal can blur the wall and overlay a logo on a timer. Cards stay 6×4. Open /wall.

Live collage — photos float and swap during the event.

Backdrop (behind the wall)

Sits in the middle of the wall under the photos. Guests glimpse the brand when tiles swap or lift. Click Upload after choosing a file.

Brand partner reveal

Periodically blurs the live wall and shows the partner logo on top. Turn this off to disable the overlay entirely. The small header chip next to inmoment still uses the same logo.
