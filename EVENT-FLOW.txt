Haunted Hoops automatic event flow

Deploy the moments-server branch before starting the matching Event build.
Existing live settings are preserved (currently 4 shots, 200 points, Normal).
New installations default to those values. The two-photo price is CAD $10.

Guest flow
- Scan the QR shown by the game, enter name and date of birth, and consent.
  Email is optional. The page reserves a turn for that kiosk.
- When called, stand on the shooting marker and confirm on the phone.
  Put the phone away: the sensor starts the game when exactly one person is
  stably inside the marker zone. Raise a shooting hand, then follow the demo.
- Complete four scored shots. Below target: rejoin the queue free, starting at
  zero. Reaching the target unlocks a purchase offer for two photos.
- The host scans the existing private player pass, records the CAD $10 payment
  with the same payment acknowledgement and Game PIN, then redeems the photos.
  There are no paid extensions in event mode.

Operations
- Unclaimed invitations expire after 45 seconds; guests can rejoin from the
  same phone. Waiting entries expire after 10 minutes. Arrival and signage
  absence timers are configurable under Game admin > System.
- F9 cancels an unfinished free game without creating a paid credit. Completed
  results remain durable and are delivered before reset.
- The game keeps the existing calibration, music and launcher settings.
  Calibrate the shooting marker at the installation before opening the queue.
  With no saved center, the fallback zone is centered 2.2 metres from the sensor,
  +/-0.6 metres horizontally and +/-0.8 metres in depth.
- Signage uses the live portrait manifest, with a verified local download and
  a bundled video fallback. A missing/stale sensor keeps the game invitation
  visible rather than assuming the space is empty.
- Real sensor placement, multiple nearby spectators, HDMI display behaviour and
  physical photo collection still require an onsite acceptance run.

Compatibility
- Existing rounds keep their original rules and existing paid balances/history
  are retained. Older kiosks cannot spend credits under the new event rules.
- Legacy paid extensions are retained only as historical data / already active
  round recovery, not sold or carried into new free games.
