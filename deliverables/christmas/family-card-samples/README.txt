Christmas family card samples

01-sources: ten different fictional generated people, including children, a teenager, parents and grandparents. Varied poses; no mugs.
02-cutouts: actual local BiRefNet background-removal outputs.
03-cards: final postcards for every count from 1 to 10.
all-counts.jpg: counts 1–5 across the top; 6–10 across the bottom.
ten-person-card.jpg: full-resolution ten-person example.
source-contact-sheet.jpg: the ten individual source portraits.

All cards use the portrait 4 x 6 ratio (1800 x 2700 pixels, 450 dpi).
Six portraits use 2 columns x 3 rows. Shared decorated evergreen divisions have no burgundy gaps or doubled frames.
The card background has a faint snowflake/star pattern; the headings use bundled Great Vibes script.

These are synthetic demonstration subjects, not photographs of a real family.
